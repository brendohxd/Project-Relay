How Relay works Live project status
Why Relay exists

AI can produce more answers than one person can responsibly inspect.

Relay is the coordination layer between AI tools, reviewers, and the person accountable for the result. It keeps the question, supporting evidence, disagreement, and final human decision connected—even when the work crosses models or applications.

The rule

More agreement is not the same as more truth. Relay makes the path to a decision inspectable.

What works today

A measured system, not a promise.

01

One accountable record

Tasks, events, evidence, reviews, and decisions have stable identifiers, deterministic hashes, and causal history.

02

Evidence without execution

Methods, commands, environments, artifacts, failures, and hashes can travel with a claim without Relay executing them.

03

Disagreement stays visible

Independent review and reproduction are explicit. Contradictions trigger remediation; they are not silently averaged away.

04

Humans keep authority

Models can contribute and compare. A named person still accepts, remediates, rejects, or defers consequential work.
